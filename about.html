Our Story

Built on a Single Promise: We Deliver

SwiftCargo Logistics started in 2001 with two trucks, one warehouse and a simple belief — that shipping should be honest, transparent and stress-free. From that small Miami depot, we grew into a global logistics partner moving freight across 190+ countries.

Today we operate a modern fleet, a network of partner carriers and 1,200+ dedicated professionals. But the promise hasn't changed: when you hand us your cargo, we treat it like our own and we deliver.

  • 1,200+ team members

  • ISO 9001 certified
